MotiMate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

MotiMate ("we", "us") is a fitness and social-motivation app that helps people find activities near them, track their own activity, and stay motivated together. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We keep it to what the app actually needs to work.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address, collected when you sign in with your MotiMate account (authentication is handled by Microsoft Entra External ID). Used to create and identify your account.
  • Location — approximate and, if you allow it, precise location. Used to show activities and people near you and to record GPS-tracked routes when you choose to track a session. Location is only accessed while you are using a feature that needs it, and only after you grant permission.
  • Photos and camera — photos you choose to add to a check-in or as a profile picture. Photos you attach to an activity check-in are shared with other people in that activity (see "Sharing" below).
  • Activity data — the activities you subscribe to, check-ins, achievements, streaks, and wellness notes you enter. Used to power your profile, progress, and personalised suggestions.
  • Notifications token — a device push token, used to deliver notifications you have opted into.
  • Basic diagnostics — limited technical logs used to keep the app reliable and diagnose errors.

MotiMate does not collect payment information in this version — premium is not yet available for purchase.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core app: sign-in, nearby activities, check-ins, tracking, and your profile.
  • To generate personalised suggestions and coaching. To do this, your activity summary (for example activity counts and your stated goal) may be processed by our AI provider to produce text suggestions. We do not send your name, email, or photos for this purpose.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

Sharing

Some content is social by design: photos and messages you add to an activity are visible to other people participating in that activity. Aside from that, we do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

We use trusted service providers to run the app, who process data on our behalf: Microsoft Azure (hosting, database, and file storage), Microsoft Entra External ID (sign-in), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering notifications), and an AI provider (generating text suggestions from activity summaries).

Data retention

We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data associated with it, except where we must keep something to meet a legal obligation.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn location, camera/photos, and notification permissions on or off at any time in your device settings.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

Children

MotiMate is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will change the "last updated" date above when we do.
