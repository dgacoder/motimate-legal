Delete your MotiMate account and data

Last updated: 10 September 2026

You can ask us to delete your MotiMate account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, and what happens.

How to request deletion

Email doguarslan.dev@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my MotiMate account".

We'll verify the request and delete your account. Please allow up to 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, location, goal).
  • Your activity data — subscriptions, check-ins, achievements, streaks, and wellness notes.
  • Photos you uploaded (profile picture and check-in photos).

What we may keep

  • Records we're legally required to retain, and minimal logs needed for security and fraud prevention, kept only as long as necessary and then deleted.
  • Content that is inherently shared and no longer tied to your identity once your account is removed (for example, an anonymous contribution to an activity's aggregate count).

Contact

Questions about deletion or your data: doguarslan.dev@gmail.com
