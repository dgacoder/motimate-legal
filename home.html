MOTIMATE

Movement is easier together.

A fitness and social-motivation app for finding what is happening near you.

Most fitness apps track you on your own. MotiMate is built around the opposite idea: that the reason people keep going is other people. It shows you what is actually happening around you right now, and makes it a single tap to join in.

What you can do

  • See what is on near you — activities and sessions happening locally, with real headcounts rather than invented ones.
  • Join in one tap — subscribe to an activity, check in when you turn up, and see who else did.
  • Propose your own — set up a one-off meetup or a recurring session and let people nearby find it.
  • Keep your streak — track your own sessions, routes, and achievements, whether or not anyone else shows up.
  • Look after the rest of it — guided breathing, stress check-ins, and nutrition logging alongside the activity side.

Availability

MotiMate is in testing on Android, with iOS to follow. If you are taking part in the test programme, you will have received a link directly.
